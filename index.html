About

I'm a graduate business analyst based in Melbourne. Much of my focus sits early in a project, where the priority is confirming the right problem is being solved before any solution is built. That means listening carefully to stakeholders, mapping how work actually flows, and writing requirements clear enough for both the analysis and the eventual build to rely on.

During my Master of Business Analytics I completed more than thirty projects across requirements engineering, process modelling, SQL analytics, dashboard development and decision analytics. I've applied this in practice through a business analytics internship with Maroondah City Council and an industry project with World Vision Australia.

I'm comfortable working across both the business and technical side of a project, whether that's BPMN and UML modelling or analysis in SQL, Power BI, Tableau and Python. Whatever the tool, I try to finish each piece of work with a clear recommendation a stakeholder can act on.

An analysis is only finished when someone can make a better decision because of it.

Queensland School Staffing Ratio Analysis

Nous Group Virtual Experience · ABS Data

Question. How does the student-to-teacher ratio in Queensland's government secondary schools compare with other sectors in the state and with government schools in NSW and Victoria?

Approach. Cleaned and reshaped 20 years of ABS schools data (2006–2025) and built two governing-thought-led slides comparing Queensland's government secondary ratio against other sectors, and against NSW and Victoria.

Data & BI

Luxuri Employee Underpayment Analysis

KordaMentha Virtual Experience · Payroll Data

Question. How much did a construction firm actually owe its workforce in unpaid wages, ahead of litigation, and could the underlying data even answer that reliably?

Approach. Profiled and cleansed a 171,046-row payroll dataset, correcting a placeholder "Employee 0" cohort, mixed date formats and a 3-column rotation error affecting 249 transactions, then applied the client's reference underpayment rates by staff type and pay category.

Outcome. Estimated total underpayment of $52.09M across 1,048 employees (11.5% of $453.36M paid), with staff employees carrying the highest exposure at $32.1M (13.0%), summarised in a client-ready executive summary.
